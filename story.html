Our Story

Not an anniversary. Not a timeline for the world.
Just the story of how you became so important to me.

01

The Beginning

“You didn't know it then...”

You were my cousin. Back then, you didn't seem interested in any of this. You were usually busy scrolling your phone, watching TV, playing cricket or going out with your friends.

Whenever I came to your house, my mom, mamayya, atthamma, cousins and grandmother would all be talking together. You would just be there, in the same room.

Sometimes you randomly looked at me. Sometimes you asked for the TV remote, asked me to switch on the fan, or asked for water.

Those tiny moments made me strangely happy.

You didn't know it then... but I was already noticing you.

02

The Memories

Distance became our little world.

I liked you for three years. I loved you secretly for six. And somehow, I'm still loving you.

When I proposed to you and you accepted me, I genuinely felt like I had been born again.

July 6, 2024 · 11:09 PM

You were in Wardhannapet. I was in Madikonda. We had late-night calls, secret conversations, waiting, missing and so many cute little talks.

We couldn't always be beside each other, so we built our own world through a phone.

03

The Difficult Days

Even silence couldn't erase us.

Our story wasn't always easy. There were misunderstandings, days with no calls, no texts, and quiet moments when both of us were hurting.

We struggled financially too, and for a long time life made things heavier than we wanted.

I missed celebrating your birthday in 2024 and 2025 the way I wanted to. I missed being beside you on some special days.

But I don't want our story to be remembered for the birthdays we missed.

I want to think about your 2027 birthday, and the one after that, and all the birthdays still waiting for us.

04

The Beautiful Days

First, my friend. Then, my love.

You accepted me as your friend when I asked you first.

And then, you accepted me as your love when I was the first to confess my feelings to you.

I’ll love you the same way I did then — today, tomorrow, and forever, Bava.

Two little yeses that changed my whole world.

Our story is still being written. ♥
